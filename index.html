My dearest Chylle,

Happy 11th monthsary, my love. I made this little corner of the internet just for you — because eleven months with you deserves more than a simple text message.

I think about the way you smile when we're out eating, the dimple that shows up when you're happy, the way you lean your head on your hand when you're being cute without even trying. I think about our beach nights, the warm lights, your starfish necklace, and how peaceful you looked like the whole world could wait for us.

I think about graduation day — you beside me, making me laugh even when I was supposed to be serious. I think about every restaurant, every shared meal, every silly moment with forks held up like we were celebrating something bigger than fast food. Because we were. We always are.

You are my calm and my excitement. My best friend and the person I want to tell everything to first. You make me want to be better, kinder, and more present — not because you ask me to, but because loving you makes me want to give you the best version of me.

I don't know how I got this lucky. But I know this: every day I choose you, and every day that choice feels easier and more right than the last. I love the way you laugh. I love the way you care. I love the way you look at me like I'm someone worth staying for.

Eleven months down — and I'd relive every single one of them with you. Here's to all the months ahead, all the dates, all the sunsets, all the ordinary mornings that become special just because you're in them.

I love you so much, Chylle.
Today, tomorrow, and every month after that.
Always yours.

With all my love, ♥ Forever & always
